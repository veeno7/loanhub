Rate and fee disclosures

APRs shown are the lowest available for the product and assume excellent credit, a 36-month term and autopay enrollment. Your rate may be higher. Maximum APR is 29.99%. Late payment fee: the lesser of 5% of the past-due amount or $15. Returned payment fee: $15. There is no prepayment penalty. Loan amounts, terms and availability vary by state. All loans are subject to credit approval. Example: a $10,000 loan at 8.99% APR over 36 months has a monthly payment of $318.00 and a total cost of $11,448.00.
